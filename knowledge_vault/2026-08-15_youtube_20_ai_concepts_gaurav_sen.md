## 📚 Key Concept Summaries

1. **LLM:** Next-token predictor neural network trained on vast web corpora.
2. **Tokenization:** Numerical encoding of words/subwords into indices (BPE).
3. **Vectors:** Multi-dimensional semantic coordinates where meaning distance is preserved.
4. **Attention:** Dynamic weighted mechanism allowing words to cross-reference dependencies across sequences.
5. **Transformers:** Parallelized attention + feed-forward architecture replacing sequential RNNs.
6. **Self-Supervised Learning:** Training on unlabeled data via masked language prediction.
7. **Fine-Tuning:** Supervised alignment of base models on domain-specific question-answer pairs.
8. **Chain of Thought (CoT):** Step-by-step intermediate reasoning before emitting final answer.
9. **Reasoning Models (LRMs):** Test-time compute allocation for self-correction and search verification.
10. **RLHF:** Human preference scoring to align AI with safety and helpfulness goals.
11. **Few-Shot Prompting:** In-context exemplars guiding output formatting without gradient updates.
12. **RAG:** Dynamic knowledge retrieval injecting fresh external facts into prompt context.
13. **Vector Databases:** Indexing systems optimized for fast Approximate Nearest Neighbor (ANN) search.
14. **MCP & Tools:** Standardized interfaces enabling AI to invoke external functions, databases, and APIs.
15. **Context Engineering:** Strategic structuring of history, system prompts, and retrieved chunks.
16. **AI Agents:** Closed-loop goal-driven systems combining planning, memory, tools, and reflection.
17. **Multimodal Models:** Unified networks processing text, audio, images, and video natively.
18. **Small Language Models (SLMs):** Lightweight models (e.g. Phi-3, Llama 8B) for fast edge deployment.
19. **Distillation:** Compressing knowledge from large teacher models into compact student models.
20. **Quantization:** Reducing numerical precision (FP16 -> INT4) to enable local device inference.

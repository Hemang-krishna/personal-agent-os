### ⚡ Skill 1: Model Context Protocol (MCP)
* **What You Learn:** How to give LLMs standardized tool-calling interfaces to access databases, local terminal files, and external APIs (Anthropic MCP standard).
* **Recommended YouTube Channels & Videos:**
  * **IndyDevDan:** [IndyDevDan YouTube Channel](https://www.youtube.com/@indydevdan) — *Model Context Protocol (MCP) Full Guide & Architecture*
  * **Dave Ebbelaar:** [Dave Ebbelaar YouTube Channel](https://www.youtube.com/@daveebbelaar) — *Building Custom MCP Servers with Python*
  * **Anthropic Official:** [Anthropic YouTube Channel](https://www.youtube.com/@AnthropicAI) — *Introducing the Model Context Protocol*

### 🤖 Skill 2: AI Agents & Autonomous Multi-Agent Swarms
* **What You Learn:** How to build autonomous reasoning loops, memory structures, and multi-agent coordination using **LangGraph, CrewAI, and AutoGen**.
* **Recommended YouTube Channels & Videos:**
  * **Brandon Hancock:** [Brandon Hancock YouTube Channel](https://www.youtube.com/@bhancockio) — *LangGraph Complete Course: Build Multi-Agent AI Teams*

### ⚙️ Skill 3: AI Automation & Low-Code Workflows (n8n & Voice)

* **Recommended YouTube Channels & Videos:**

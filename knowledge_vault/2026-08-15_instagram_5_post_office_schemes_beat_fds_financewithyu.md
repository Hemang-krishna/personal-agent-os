### 🖼️ Slide 2: Scheme 1 — Post Office Monthly Income Scheme (POMIS)
* **Core Value Proposition:**
  * Interest paid directly into your bank account every single month.
  * 100% Government-backed sovereign investment.
  * Ideal for retirees, freelancers, and passive income seekers looking for guaranteed monthly cashflow.
* **Key Specifications:**
  * **Interest Rate:** **7.4% p.a.** (Paid Monthly)
  * **Tenure:** 5 Years
  * **Minimum Investment:** ₹1,000
  * **Maximum Investment Limit:** ₹9 Lakh (Single Account), ₹15 Lakh (Joint Account)
  * **Best For:** Investors seeking predictable, monthly dividend-style income without market volatility.

---

### 🖼️ Slide 3: Scheme 2 — Senior Citizen Savings Scheme (SCSS)
* **Core Value Proposition:**
  * Highest sovereign fixed-income interest rate for senior citizens.
  * Quarterly interest payouts credited directly on the 1st of April, July, October, and January.
  * Safe, reliable income stream with zero credit default risk.
* **Key Specifications:**
  * **Interest Rate:** **8.2% p.a.** (Paid Quarterly)
  * **Tenure:** 5 Years (Extendable by an additional 3-year block)
  * **Minimum Investment:** ₹1,000
  * **Maximum Investment Limit:** ₹30 Lakh
  * **Eligibility:** Individuals aged 60+ (55+ for individuals retiring under voluntary VRS rules).
  * **Tax Benefits:** Eligible for **Section 80C tax deduction up to ₹1.5 Lakh/year**.

---

### 🖼️ Slide 4: Scheme 3 — National Savings Certificate (NSC)
* **Core Value Proposition:**
  * Fixed, guaranteed return backed by the Government of India.
  * Interest is compounded annually and reinvested automatically, paying out as a lump sum at maturity.
  * Can be pledged as collateral/security to secure bank loans.
* **Key Specifications:**
  * **Interest Rate:** **7.7% p.a.** (Compounded Annually, Paid at Maturity)
  * **Tenure:** 5 Years
  * **Minimum Investment:** ₹1,000
  * **Maximum Investment Limit:** **No Upper Limit**
  * **Tax Benefits:** Eligible for **Section 80C tax deduction up to ₹1.5 Lakh/year** (Accrued interest for the first 4 years is also deemed reinvested and eligible for 80C).

---

### 🖼️ Slide 5: Scheme 4 — Public Provident Fund (PPF)
* **Core Value Proposition:**
  * The gold standard of Indian retail fixed-income: **Triple-E (EEE) Tax Status** (Investment is tax-exempt, interest is tax-exempt, maturity proceeds are 100% tax-free).
  * Cannot be attached by any court decree or creditor claims.
* **Key Specifications:**
  * **Interest Rate:** **7.1% p.a.** (Compounded Annually)
  * **Tenure:** 15 Years (Extendable indefinitely in blocks of 5 years with or without contributions)
  * **Minimum Investment:** ₹500 per fiscal year
  * **Maximum Investment Limit:** ₹1.5 Lakh per fiscal year
  * **Liquidity:** Partial withdrawal allowed from 7th year; loan facility available from 3rd to 6th fiscal year.

---

### 🖼️ Slide 6: Scheme 5 — Kisan Vikas Patra (KVP) & Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY)
* **Kisan Vikas Patra (KVP):**
  * **Interest Rate:** **7.5% p.a.** (Compounded Annually)
  * **Doubling Period:** Doubles your invested money in **115 Months** (9 Years & 7 Months).
  * **Minimum Investment:** ₹1,000 (No maximum limit).
* **Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY) — Special Girl Child Scheme:**
  * **Interest Rate:** **8.2% p.a.** (Compounded Annually, 100% Tax-Free EEE).
  * **Eligibility:** Parents/guardians of girl children aged up to 10 years.
  * **Tenure:** 21 Years from opening (Deposits required for first 15 years).
  * **Limits:** Minimum ₹250/year, Maximum ₹1.5 Lakh/year.
* **5-Year Post Office Time Deposit (TD):**
  * **Interest Rate:** **7.5% p.a.** (Calculated quarterly, paid annually).
  * **Tax Benefit:** 5-Year TD qualifies for Section 80C tax rebate.

### 🖼️ Slide 7: Summary & Comparison: Post Office vs. Bank Fixed Deposits

| Comparison Metric | Post Office Savings Schemes | Commercial Bank Fixed Deposits |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sovereign Safety** | **100% Sovereign Guarantee** by Govt of India (Zero Default Risk) | Insured only up to ₹5 Lakh per bank by DICGC |
| **Interest Rate Range** | **7.1% to 8.2% p.a.** | Typically 6.5% to 7.2% p.a. |
| **Tax Exemption** | **EEE Status on PPF & SSY** (100% Tax-Free) | Fully Taxable as per investor income tax slab |
| **Senior Citizen Yield** | **8.2% p.a.** on SCSS (Quarterly Payout) | 7.0% to 7.7% p.a. |
| **Monthly Income** | **7.4% p.a.** on POMIS (Guaranteed Monthly Cashflow) | Usually lower on monthly payout options |

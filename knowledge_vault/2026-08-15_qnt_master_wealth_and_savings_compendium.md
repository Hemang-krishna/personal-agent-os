## PART 1: 3 WAYS TO OPEN POST OFFICE SAVINGS SCHEMES (@IAMNEHANAGAR)

**[METHOD 1] Offline In-Branch Account Opening**

- **Step 1:** Visit your nearest Head Post Office (HPO) or Sub Post Office (SO).
- **Step 2:** Request and complete Form-1 (Account Opening Form).
- **Step 3 (KYC Checklist):** Aadhaar Card, PAN Card (mandatory >₹50k), Passport/Voter ID, 2 Passport Photos.
- **Step 4:** Deposit minimum initial funds via Cash/Cheque. Physical passbook issued on the spot. **✨ Sovereign Advantage: Instant physical verification with zero digital dependency.** **[METHOD 2] Online via India Post Internet Banking (ebanking.indiapost.gov.in)**
- **Step 1:** Ensure active Post Office Savings Account (POSA) linked to your mobile.
- **Step 2:** Navigate to *ebanking.indiapost.gov.in* ──► Click "New User Activation" ──► Enter CIF ID & Account No.
- **Step 3:** Log in ──► Go to **"General Services"** ──► **"Service Requests"** ──► **"New Requests"**.
- **Step 4:** Select scheme (PPF, Time Deposit, RD), enter deposit amount, and authorize with Transaction Password + OTP. **[METHOD 3] Online via India Post / IPPB Mobile Banking App**
- **Step 1:** Download the official India Post Mobile Banking / IPPB App from Play Store / App Store.
- **Step 2:** Register using CIF ID, Account Number, DOB, and Mobile OTP ──► Set 4-digit MPIN.
- **Step 3:** Tap on the **"Requests"** / **"Investments"** tab ──► Select scheme (PPF, RD, TD).
- **Step 4:** Confirm tenure and debit amount ──► Enter MPIN to open account instantly with digital receipt.

## PART 2: 5 POST OFFICE SCHEMES THAT BEAT FIXED DEPOSITS (@FINANCEWITHYU)

**[SCHEME 01] Post Office Monthly Income Scheme (POMIS) — 7.4% p.a.**

- **Payout Frequency:** Monthly direct credit.
- **Limits & Tenure:** ₹1,000 Min | ₹9 Lakh Max (Single), ₹15 Lakh Max (Joint) | 5-Year Tenure.
- **Target Investor:** Retirees, freelancers, and passive income seekers looking for guaranteed monthly cashflow. **[SCHEME 02] Senior Citizen Savings Scheme (SCSS) — 8.2% p.a.**
- **Payout Frequency:** Quarterly interest credit (April 1, July 1, Oct 1, Jan 1).
- **Limits & Eligibility:** ₹1,000 Min | ₹30 Lakh Max | 5-Year Tenure (extendable by 3 years) | Age 60+ (55+ for VRS).
- **Tax Benefit:** Section 80C tax deduction up to ₹1.5 Lakh/year. **[SCHEME 03] National Savings Certificate (NSC) — 7.7% p.a.**
- **Compounding:** Compounded annually, paid at maturity.
- **Limits & Collateral:** ₹1,000 Min | **No Upper Limit** | 5-Year Tenure | Accepted as bank loan collateral.
- **Tax Benefit:** Section 80C tax deduction up to ₹1.5 Lakh/year. **[SCHEME 04] Public Provident Fund (PPF) — 7.1% p.a. (Triple-E Tax Free)**
- **Tax Status: EEE (Exempt-Exempt-Exempt)** — 100% Tax-Free investment, interest, and maturity proceeds.
- **Limits & Tenure:** ₹500 Min | ₹1.5 Lakh Max / fiscal year | 15-Year Tenure (extendable in 5-year blocks).
- **Asset Protection:** Immune from court attachment or creditor claims. **[SCHEME 05] Kisan Vikas Patra (KVP) & Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY)**
- **KVP:** 7.5% p.a. compounded annually | Doubles capital in **115 Months (9y 7m)** | ₹1,000 Min (No limit).
- **SSY: 8.2% p.a.** (100% Tax-Free EEE) | For girl child up to 10 years | ₹250 Min, ₹1.5L Max / year | 21-Year Tenure.
- **5-Year Time Deposit (TD):** 7.5% p.a. (compounded quarterly) | Eligible for Section 80C rebate.

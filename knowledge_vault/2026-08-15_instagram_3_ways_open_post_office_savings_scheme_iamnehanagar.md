### 🏤 Method 1: The Offline In-Branch Procedure
1. **Visit Branch:** Go to your nearest authorized Head Post Office (HPO) or Sub Post Office (SO).
2. **Form Collection:** Request Form-1 (Account Opening Form) for the desired scheme (e.g. PPF, SCSS, NSC, TD, RD).
3. **KYC Documents Required:**
   * Identity Proof (Aadhaar Card, Passport, Voter ID)
   * Address Proof (Aadhaar Card, Utility Bill)
   * PAN Card (Mandatory for investments above ₹50,000)
   * 2 Passport-sized photographs
4. **Deposit & Passbook:** Deposit the initial minimum balance via Cash, Cheque, or DD. Receive your physical passbook on the spot.

---

### 🌐 Method 2: Online via India Post Internet Banking Portal
1. **Prerequisite:** Must have an active Post Office Savings Account (POSA) with registered mobile number.
2. **Access Portal:** Navigate to the official portal: `https://ebanking.indiapost.gov.in`.
3. **Activation:** Click **"New User Activation"** → Enter your **Customer ID (CIF)** and **Account Number**.
4. **Login & Request:**
   * Log in with User ID and Password.
   * Navigate to **"General Services"** ──► **"Service Requests"** ──► **"New Requests"**.
   * Select **"Open a New Scheme Account"** (e.g. PPF, National Savings Time Deposit, Recurring Deposit).
   * Specify tenure, nominee details, and initial transfer amount.
   * Authenticate via Transaction Password & SMS OTP.

---

### 📲 Method 3: Via India Post Mobile Banking Application
1. **Download App:** Install the official **"India Post Mobile Banking"** or **IPPB Mobile Banking** app from Google Play Store or Apple App Store.
2. **First-Time Setup:** Register using Account Number, CIF ID, Date of Birth, and Mobile OTP → Set a 4-digit MPIN.
3. **Account Creation Flow:**
   * Tap on **"Requests"** or **"Investments"** tab.
   * Choose target scheme: **PPF Account**, **Recurring Deposit (RD)**, or **Time Deposit (TD)**.
   * Select debit account, deposit amount, and tenure.
   * Tap **Confirm** → Enter MPIN. Account opens instantly with real-time digital receipt.

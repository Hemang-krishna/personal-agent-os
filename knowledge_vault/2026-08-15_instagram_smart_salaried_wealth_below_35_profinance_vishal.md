### 🖼️ Slide 6: Tax Optimization Blueprint (Old vs. New Regime)
* **National Pension System (NPS):**
  * Additional ₹50,000 deduction under **Section 80CCD(1B)** over and above Section 80C.
* **Equity Linked Savings Scheme (ELSS):**
  * Shortest lock-in period (3 Years) among all 80C instruments with high historical equity returns.
* **Health Insurance (Section 80D):**
  * Up to ₹25,000 for self & family + ₹50,000 for senior citizen parents.
* **New Tax Regime Strategy:** Focus on maximizing pure post-tax equity compounding if deductions under Old Regime do not exceed ₹3.75 Lakh.

### 🖼️ Slide 7: Beating Real Inflation in India
* **Nominal vs. Real Inflation:**
  * Official CPI index reflects ~4.5%–5.5%, but real lifestyle inflation (Education, Healthcare, Urban Rent) runs at **7%–10% p.a.**
* **Why Fixed Deposits Fail Long-Term Wealth Creation:**
  * Bank FD @ 7.0% − 30% Tax Slab = **4.9% Post-Tax Return**.
  * Net Real Return = $4.9\% - 7.0\% = \mathbf{-2.1\%}$ (Capital shrinks in purchasing power).
* **Equity Compounding:** Nifty 50 historical 15-year CAGR of **12%–14%** generates positive real alpha of **+5% to +7% p.a.**

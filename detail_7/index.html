The 7 Stages of PPF & Ceramic Protocol

Every vehicle admitted into 7Theory undergoes an uncompromised 7-stage clinical protocol. No skips, no shortcuts.

01
DIAGNOSIS
Defect Mapping & Paint Audit

Full vehicle inspection under high-CRI LED swirl-finder lighting. Ultrasonic digital paint depth gauges map clearcoat thickness across every millimeter. Panel-by-panel digital defect logging before touching the finish.

🔍 Web Cue: High-Res Defect Scan & Paint Depth Telemetry
STAGE 01: LED SWIRL SCAN
02
DECONTAMINATION
Chemical Fallout & Clay Treatment

Multi-stage pH-neutral snow foam bath, active iron-dissolving chemical decon to purge embedded brake dust, tar removal solvent bath, and fine grade synthetic clay bar glide to restore glass-smooth virgin clearcoat.

🧪 Web Cue: Chemical Iron Dissolution & Clean Slate Texture
STAGE 02: VIRGIN CLEARCOAT
03
CORRECTION
Dual-Action Machine Jewel Polish

Rotary & Random Orbital machine polishing utilizing diminishing abrasive micro-compounds. Leveling out orange peel, eliminating RIDS (random isolated deep scratches), and restoring true optical gloss and deep reflection depth.

✨ Web Cue: 50/50 Before-After Mirror Gloss Slider
STAGE 03: 100% GLOSS REFLECTION
04
PRECISION FIT
CAD Digital Pattern Plotting

Panel-specific PPF templates cut via laser-guided Graphtec plotters from proprietary CAD geometric files. Custom wrapped edges eliminate razor blades anywhere near painted surfaces for a bespoke factory appearance.

📐 Web Cue: Technical Blueprint Wireframe Overlays
STAGE 04: CAD PATTERN PLOT
05
APPLICATION
Hydro-Gel Wet Install & Edge Tuck

Purified slip solution wet installation under positive-pressure cleanroom conditions. Specialized squeegee expulsion of micro-bubbles, followed by thermal heat-forming around complex curves and panel recesses.

💧 Web Cue: Positive-Pressure Cleanroom Bay Process Loop
STAGE 05: SEAMLESS WRAPPED EDGES
06
CERAMIC LAYERING
Multi-Coat SiO2 / SiC Molecular Bond

Application of multi-layer 9H/10H ceramic coatings over film and exposed surfaces. Controlled infrared short-wave baking intervals ensure maximum molecular cross-linking for extreme hydrophobicity and UV rejection.

🛡️ Web Cue: 110° Water-Contact Angle Caustic Beading
STAGE 06: MOLECULAR INFRARED CURE
07
CERTIFICATION
UV Quality Control & Warranty Handover

Final UV blacklight quality control inspection, paint gloss meter verification, registration on the 7Theory Global Warranty Database, and physical issuance of the 7Theory Certified Aluminum Plaque and Aftercare Kit.

🏅 Web Cue: '7 Theory Certified' Digital & Physical Hologram
